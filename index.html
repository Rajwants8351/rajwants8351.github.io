❓ Why Convert SVG to STL?

  • Design Freedom: Create custom 3D models from simple 2D drawings
  • Easy Customization: Modify designs in any vector software (like Inkscape or Illustrator)
  • Print-Ready: STL files work with most 3D printers and slicing software
  • Professional Results: Extrusion adds depth and dimension to any flat design

✨ Pro Tips for Best Results

  • Use SVG files with clear, simple paths for best extrusion results
  • Check your SVG files with the pre-flight tool before converting
  • Keep your designs between 50-500mm for optimal printing size
  • Learn about different extrusion depths to create various 3D effects
